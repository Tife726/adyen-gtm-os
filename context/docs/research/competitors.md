## How Adyen positions against them (its own page)
- Adyen stresses in-house terminals (16 options) and dedicated account management versus Checkout.com and Worldpay. Source: https://www.adyen.com/en_GB/knowledge-hub/stripe-alternatives (checked 2026-11-13)

## Review scores (G2, small samples)
- Adyen 4.0 (44 reviews), Checkout.com 4.6 (71), Stripe Connect 4.3 (249), Worldpay 2.9 (42, 75% small business). Sources: https://www.g2.com/compare/adyen-payments-vs-checkout-com-vs-stripe-connect , https://www.g2.com/compare/adyen-vs-worldpay (checked 2026-11-13)
- Trustpilot: Adyen TrustScore 1.3 from 451 reviews (mostly sub-merchants and payees, not direct enterprise customers: INFERRED). Source: treg crawl4ai.trustpilot (checked 2026-11-13)

## Volume
- Adyen passed PayPal in payments volume (headline). Source: https://nanalyze.com/2026/10/adyen-surpasses-paypal-in-payments-volume/ (checked 2026-11-13)
- Common setup: one primary processor at 80-90% of volume plus a backup for failover. Source: https://costbench.com/best/best-payment-processor-for-enterprise/ (checked 2026-11-13)
