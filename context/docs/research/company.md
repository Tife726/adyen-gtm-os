# Adyen: company research (raw)
All facts checked 2026-11-13 unless noted. INFERRED = my inference.

## What they sell
- Their own description: "One platform for payments, data, and financial products. Built to scale with the world's leading businesses." Source: https://www.adyen.com/ (checked 2026-11-13)
- Product names on the homepage: Intelligent Money Movement, Embedded Finance, Adyen for Platforms, Unified Payments, Risk management, Authentication, Issuing. Source: https://www.adyen.com/ (checked 2026-11-13)
- Intelligent Money Movement (launched 9 Apr 2026) connects payments, liquidity management and payouts. It is aimed at insurance, retail marketplaces, mobility and delivery platforms, and OTAs. The first rollout includes Etsy, Expedia Group and Vinted. Source: https://www.adyen.com/press-and-media/adyen-launches-intelligent-money-movement (checked 2026-11-13)
- Uplift: in Feb 2026 Adyen expanded it with Personalize, which adapts the checkout in real time. H1 2026: conversion up 0.9pp on average through Uplift and Dynamic Identification. Source: https://www.adyen.com/press-and-media/adyen-publishes-h1-2026-financial-results-3wjne (checked 2026-11-13)
- Adyen Agentic is a suite of modular APIs for selling through conversational AI platforms. Launch date: Not found. Source: https://whitesight.net/decoding-ai-dyens-strategy-from-payment-optimisation-to-agentic-infrastructure/ (checked 2026-11-13)
- Acquisitions Orb and Talon.One added about 2pp to Q3 2026 constant-currency growth (secondary source). Source: https://www.fxcintel.com/research/analysis/adyen-q3-2026-earnings (checked 2026-11-13)

## Pricing model
- Interchange++: "a fixed processing fee plus a fee determined by the payment method". Source: https://www.adyen.com/pricing (checked 2026-11-13)
- UK list rate: £0.11 + Interchange + 0.60%. US: $0.13 + interchange++ + 0.60% for Visa/MC (NerdWallet). Sources: https://www.adyen.com/en_GB/pricing , https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/learn/adyen (checked 2026-11-13)
- Third parties report a minimum monthly invoice. The amount is disputed (€100 vs ~€1,000). Source: https://www.withorb.com/pricing-atlas/adyen (checked 2026-11-13)
- A third party says it typically requires $10M+ in annual volume. Source: https://costbench.com/best/best-payment-processor-for-enterprise/ (checked 2026-11-13)
