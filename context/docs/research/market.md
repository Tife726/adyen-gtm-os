# Market: regulation and changes that create urgency (raw)
Checked 2026-11-13.

## EU PSD3 / PSR
- Political deal 27 Nov 2025. Final compromise texts published 23 Apr 2026. Not yet in force. Expected in the Official Journal in H2 2026, though estimates range to Jan 2027. Source: https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/260430-psd3-and-the-payment-services-regulation-key-developments (checked 2026-11-13)
- The PSR applies about 21 months after entry into force. It covers SCA, fraud liability, open banking and transparency, and extends Verification of Payee. Source: https://worldline.com/en/home/main-navigation/resources/blogs/2026/the-scope-and-timeline-are-locked-in-for-psd3-and-psr-what-should-psps-know (checked 2026-11-13)
- Law firm framing: "plenty of time on paper, little time in practice". Source: https://www.addleshawgoddard.com/en/insights/insights-briefings/2026/financial-regulation/financial-regulation-in-the-know-payments-september-2026/psd3-new-era-eu-payment-services-regulation/ (checked 2026-11-13)

## Agentic commerce
- No US, EU or UK payments law written specifically for agent-made purchases (as of Oct 2026). Source: https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/agentic-commerce-regulation-what-merchants-need-to-know (checked 2026-11-13)
- Visa Intelligent Commerce and Mastercard Agent Pay are live programs. Visa Core Rules contain agentic provisions. Source: https://fbtgibbons.com/the-payment-infrastructure-layer-how-network-rules-already-govern-agentic-commerce/ (checked 2026-11-13)
- Prediction: the first binding agent dispute rule comes in the April 2027 release at the earliest. Source: https://shopappy.com/payments/card-networks/agent-chargeback-rules-holiday-2026 (checked 2026-11-13)
- Network tokenization: Visa ~60% of global ecommerce, Mastercard 40%+ (unverified secondary). Source: https://stellagent.ai/insights/visa-mastercard-agentic-commerce-strategy (checked 2026-11-13)
