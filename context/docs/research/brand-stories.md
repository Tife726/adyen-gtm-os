## 3. Patterns for ICP and signals (INFERRED from the stories above)
- **The main trigger is fragmentation.** At least 10 stories describe 3 to 6+ providers, local acquirers or gateways before the switch: Teamfront, Tessitura, Connect&GO, SKIDATA, Louboutin, Moose Knuckles, Next Commerce, Oriflame, Crocs, Lightspeed. Signal to look for: a company with multiple PSPs or acquirers across countries.
- **Second trigger: expansion into a new country or channel.** Examples: BlaBlaCar, MUJI, HEYTEA, Tinyme, Brompton's first US store, foodora (1 to 40 cities in 10 countries). Signal: new market launch or first store abroad.
- **Third trigger: a vertical SaaS platform launching embedded payments.** Examples: Teamfront (field services), Tessitura (arts), ROLLER and Connect&GO (attractions), Sport Alliance (fitness), Lightspeed and Zenchef (restaurants), Secutix (ticketing), SKIDATA (parking), Atlas (F&B), Epos Now and yetipay (SMB POS), Next Commerce, Relay. Signal: hiring a "VP/Head of Payments" at a SaaS company, or a SaaS company announcing "X Payments".
- **Other triggers:** fraud loss (PXG, Freelancer), a merger (Just Eat Takeaway), a legacy platform that can't scale (MUJI, FAO Schwarz), and pricing disputes or downtime (Rebtel).
- **Most frequent verticals:** fashion and luxury retail, hospitality and F&B, attractions and ticketing, subscriptions and streaming, marketplaces.
- **Lookalike seeds from the stories:** PXG to other DTC golf brands; DAZN to other sports streamers; Teamfront to field-service SaaS (e.g. ServiceTitan, Jobber); ROLLER to other attractions SaaS; Tessitura to other arts ticketing platforms. (INFERRED)
